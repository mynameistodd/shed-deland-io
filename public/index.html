Live from the backyard woods · Chelsea, MI

Yes, I actually work in a shed.

Whenever it comes up on a call that my office is a backyard shed, people understandably have questions. Here’s the quick visual tour: tucked into the treeline 30 seconds from the house, moved into the same afternoon it slid off the trailer, and insulated for four Michigan seasons.

Casual Conversation FAQ

What people usually ask first

⏱️

“How long did it take before you moved in?”

Four and a half hours. The shed slid off the delivery trailer onto the gravel pad at 2:00 PM on July 25, 2023. By 6:30 PM that same evening, the standing desk, dual monitors, MacBook, and mic arm were set up inside—running off a heavy-duty yellow extension cord out the barn doors.

🥶

“Isn’t it freezing in a Michigan winter?”

Not at all. Before winter hit, I packed the 2×4 stud bays and rafters with Owens Corning R-13 kraft-faced fiberglass batts and skinned the ceiling rafters with pink Foamular R-5 rigid foam board. Because the footprint is compact, a single space heater (and window A/C in July) keeps it comfortable year-round.

⚡

“How do you get power and internet out there?”

Phase one was a yellow extension cord snaked across the grass. In October 2023, we dug a trench from the main house to the shed, buried electrical conduit under the fall leaves, and wired in a dedicated Square D subpanel, overhead lighting, and rock-solid Wi-Fi from the house.

🌲

“Why a shed instead of a spare room inside?”

Physical separation. Stepping out the back door and walking 100 feet to the edge of the woods creates a real boundary between “at work” and “at home”—plus I get windows on both sides looking straight into the trees across all four seasons.
